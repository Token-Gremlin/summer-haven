# Water and nature focus — fixed scope and checks

The owner's latest instruction supersedes expansion priorities: **do not enlarge
the world**. Improve water, trees, grass and nature within existing places.
PR #3 preserves the complete prior snapshot; it has been merged by the owner.
Current local work starts from `bbb185b`, whose gameplay is `59516f8`, compiled
as `BoqztZFN`. The earlier full-world criteria remain quality references, not

## Scope fixed before production edits

- Preserve world bounds, terrain-cell coverage, named regions, landmarks,
  navigable routes, population and save format.
- Give existing rivers, paddies and waterfall a coherent painterly material
  family, readable flow and better contact with their existing banks. Correct
  duplicated surfaces, abrupt color/height seams or reflection-edge artifacts
  where reproduced. Preserve water exclusion, ferry and creature support.
- Refine existing tree silhouettes and grass/understory shapes and distribution;
  keep open paths and wildlife observation spaces. Density alone is not polish.
- No new paid service, hosting or automatic publication of the new local work.

## Frozen contribution checks

1. Matched before/after views of village river, fields, Mosswood and Silverveil,
   including bank contact and the fall's lip/impact/outlet. Capture actual build,
   camera, time, viewport, quality overrides and limits of temporal matching.
2. Inspect moving water and camera movement, with no new shader error, flashing
   surface overlap, sharp reflection clamp band or crawling fine foam. Verify
   Low and High, daylight and evening. Stills cannot approve temporal quality.
3. Preserve all region/water surface, bank-trail, roadside and habitat collision
   tests. New seam or placement tests must use actual rendered geometry and
   shared physical contracts rather than just source strings.
4. Nature authoring budgets: no extra tree/cell population, textures or shader
   samples; at most two groundcover batches per existing region cell. Low
   round/tall tree ≤1,250 triangles, low cedar ≤750, grass ≤40 and understory
   ≤34. Groundcover population must not grow. Geometry bounds include wind.
5. Compare representative populated water/forest frame intervals under matched
   conditions without screenshot or authoring overhead during measurement.
   Extra cost requires investigation; geometry budgets do not prove runtime
   neutrality. Historical 33–42 FPS is not a measurement of this candidate.
6. A fresh independent critic receives fixed criteria, references and actual
   outputs, with neutral pairs where practical. No full artistic, audio,
   performance or journey approval is inferred from a narrow contribution pass.
