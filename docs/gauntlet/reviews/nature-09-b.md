## Largest remaining visible gap and next correction

The largest remaining visible gap is **the integration of water with the existing terrain and local impact zones**. N21 corrects the worst cross-river material seam and N81 gives the pool a coherent water color. However, hard polygon shorelines, a ribbon-like upper stream, the shared noisy lip stripe and generalized surface curves still prevent the convincing bank/channel/impact hierarchy seen in the official N2 principle. Keep the successful calm teal family and clear paths; refine contact locally instead of coating every surface with foam or new high-frequency marks.

Next evidence should show the same upper-stream banks, joining surfaces, fall lip and impact zone during slow camera movement and normal water animation. Inspect the shared stripe at the lip with the camera approaching and passing it; only motion can distinguish a stable rough material edge from crawling overlap or aliasing. The static packet establishes that it predates whichever neutral image is preferred, not its cause. Likewise, capture the actual upper-water-to-falling-sheet transition and downstream outlet in connected views. Low/High, daylight/evening, populated performance measurements and practical travel checks remain separate requirements. No missing requirement is cured by the reported capture process or a test result.
