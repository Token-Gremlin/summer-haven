## Initial observations

The wide empty road converges on an empty horizontal field. Detached, similarly proportioned houses and regularly spaced trees do not establish a composed urban destination or convincingly distinct neighborhoods. Foreground canopy shadows provide more visual activity than the middle-distance street. A sign and stalls suggest a market, but purposeful activity cannot be inferred from stillness.
- **Interior02:** chart, counter, books and timber floor make a comprehensible room program, and diagonal light is inviting. Oversized flat signs dominate rather than material or prop craft. The chart labels behind the player are partly occluded; the image alone does not prove their normal reading position. The HUD says “Mori general store” despite the supplied guildhall filename. This is an observable naming mismatch, not proof of which room was actually loaded.
- **Falls03:** strong player-to-drop scale and a clear pale focal mass; however, the cliff is a huge nearly planar tan slope, and the fall resembles an evenly striped trapezoidal sheet. Crest transition, eroded channel, rock articulation and energetic receiving-pool contact are weak or absent in this view. Upstream/downstream continuity is not visible.
- **Falls04/04b:** thin isolated green-topped oval ledges project across the cliff with empty air below. They read as floating platforms rather than connected geology. This is the largest observed coherence defect. In 04b the irregular bright water streaks improve on 03's even stripes, but the smooth sheet silhouette and weak crest/impact remain. Faint haze at lower right modestly helps separation. 04 is a different camera; 04b has a comparable approach angle but differs in framing/player position, so it is not an exact pixel match. No motion claim follows from changed water patterns in separate stills.
