## Decision and largest gap

For this narrow static comparison, favor **R19 / tie R62–R83 / R25 / R56**. Relative to their partners, R48, R71, and R34 regress calm painterly water scale through conspicuous broad loop markings. This is a judgment between neutral images, not a statement about which build regressed. There is no clear full-falls regression. Trees, grasses, bank geometry, and overall layout show no material improvement established by these pairs.

The largest shared gap is **water-to-land and fall-to-pool integration**, affecting the visual portion of W5 and Q1/Q2. The receiving pool needs a stronger localized impact hierarchy; the banks still read as abrupt planar cutouts or straight colored strips. The documented N2 principle is that channels, relief, banks, and route read together. Here the channel is readable, but the contact treatment remains conspicuously geometric and the large looping highlights do not improve that relationship.

Next experiment: retain the quieter open-water treatment; concentrate the strongest pale breakup at the curtain's actual contact and reduce it over a short downstream interval. Vary existing bank contact locally with restrained shallow/wet transitions that follow the present shoreline, without adding world area or population. Recompare the same close impact and village join views, then separately inspect moving Low/High water in daylight and evening before any temporal approval.

This is a comparative still-image decision only. It does not pass W5, Q2/Q3/Q4, the nature focus as a whole, or the broad Goal.
