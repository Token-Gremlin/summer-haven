## Verdict

**Prefer R overall; neither set passes the narrow static hero-creature craft gate.** R has the more controlled folded/rest silhouette and cleaner membrane surfaces. S makes more of the wing-to-body membrane visible in extended poses, but its rest pose exposes conspicuous sheet pinching and an incompletely folded wing. This is a meaningful tradeoff, not an across-the-board upgrade. Both remain at **3 for shape/anatomy and surface craft** against the frozen requirement of 4/5. Their attractive face and coherent colors do not average away those shortcomings.

**Largest remaining gap: a convincing wing structure that reads as one anatomical mechanism in both spread and folded poses.** R avoids the worst shading defects but appears to fold a rigid miniature fan beside the shoulder. S exposes more connective membrane, yet that sheet bunches into sharp triangular ridges while the long leading limbs stay raised and spread in its supplied rest image. A membrane is not convincing solely because the region is filled.
