Camera, content, lighting and rendering differ, so this is a comparison of organization and craft, not pixel parity.

## Post-choice encounter evidence

I inspected runtime-01.png through runtime-05.png under wildlife-r2. These are five sparse images, not five observed motion intervals. They show deer distributed beyond the road, one near the player in runtime-02, lowered-head configurations in runtime-03/04 and a rear three-quarter configuration on the path in runtime-05. These prove visible pose/location variety in supplied samples. They do not prove gait, contact, reaction timing, blending, natural feeding, collision or the path taken between samples. In runtime-03 the near deer's lowered head and narrow crossed/overlapping foreleg silhouettes look awkward enough to require a close moving check; a single image cannot establish a gait defect.

I read the recording's JSON metadata and summarized all 302 state samples. It reports 75,006.5 ms, normal camera, initial diagnostic placement at 211,-500, and no player/animal/clock writes during recording. State records include rest/feed/wander for all three glade deer and react for deer 0 and 2. These are state labels, not independent visual proof of convincing activity. The supplied WebM was inspected with ffprobe: it contains a 1150×835 VP9 video stream and Opus audio, file size 12,777,341 bytes. I did **not** watch its continuous 75-second playback or listen to its audio. The available visual tool observations here were still screenshots; video metadata and an audio stream cannot substitute for M/A evidence. I did not treat the reported stream frame-rate field as measured gameplay performance. No exact timestamps are claimed for the five images because their filenames do not establish them.

## Gate decision and next experiment

Narrow decision: **retain R's static visual improvement for continued iteration**, not an L1/B2/Q2/Q3/Q4 pass. Q4 fails the threshold in multiple observed visual dimensions; animation/audio are unverified. L1 remains unverified by this review. B2's temporal/export claims and full-world gates are outside the demonstrated evidence. The complete world objective cannot be discharged by a better deer or this stationary glade.

Largest deer gap: expressive, anatomically coherent close-range form and believable weight. Next experiment: keep R's color continuity, author a more integrated eye/socket and muzzle, refine shoulder-to-upper-leg volume and hoof silhouettes, then repeat the same blind three-view comparison plus a genuinely distinct medium approach. Follow with contiguous normal-camera rest → start → walk/turn → stop → feed → react observation on level and sloped ground, with a low unobstructed contact view. A listener must separately assess the contextual audio.

Largest integrated-scene gap: repetitive habitat structure. Shape a small glade around a few irregular trunk/canopy clusters, use understory and ground-color transitions to group detail, and leave a deliberate sightline from the playable path to the deer. Compare an actual player approach before expanding this repeated forest treatment across further regions.
