The synthetic empty-corner test demonstrates a benefit over a box; it cannot prove no false retraction around production crowns. Include an under-crown start: the explicit inside case returns the 0.28 m minimum regardless of departure direction. That is intentional in the present test, not proof of comfortable presentation.
