## Comparisons for the first critic packets

1. **City:** compare old village street and new city street at equivalent player height/time/resolution; use N1 only for broad skyline/color hierarchy, N4 for readable resident posing. Require moving crowd/business evidence separately. Do not call N1 a gameplay target capture.
2. **Forest/river/waterfall:** compare a complete approach at player height and a viewpoint; N2 for land/water topology, traversable route and clustered detail; Z1/Z2 for reveal, depth and landmark separation.
3. **Temple/mountain:** use Z1/Z2 for distinctive approach silhouette and large-scale height relationships, with near-arrival views proving that the landmark is authored and reachable.
4. **Characters:** compare actual in-game dialogue distance and moving contact, with N4 as a narrow expressive-pose principle and existing close-up as preservation baseline. A character still does not certify gait.
5. **Dragons:** N3 for anatomical hierarchy, grounded scale and light separating large forms; Z2 for territory composition. None of the inspected sources provides a sufficiently reviewed dragon flight cycle: obtain and log an appropriate concrete motion reference before approving D2, while keeping the already-frozen behavioral/quality requirements.

The references are intentionally specific rather than a general “looks like Zelda” judgment. Their relevant principles must be visible in original Summer Haven work. Review packets should select the appropriate comparison, name the scene/frame, disclose mismatched rendering/platform conditions, and avoid claiming an uninspected reference property.
