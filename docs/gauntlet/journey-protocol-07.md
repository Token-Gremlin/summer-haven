## Controls and observation

- Walk: W/A/S/D or arrows relative to camera; Shift jogs (2.35/4.8 m/s outside). Drag canvas to look, wheel to change camera distance. E interacts; release E and press again, or Space, to close dialogue. Escape resumes paused/dialogue/journal screens. J opens the journal. F mounts within 2 m of the parked bicycle or dismounts where space exists; allow its .7/.65-second transition. B rings the bell.
- Cycling: W pedals, S brakes/reverses, A/D steer, Shift increases speed. Bicycle heading is **not** camera-relative. Do not use the walking helper while mounted: it steers camera only and cannot steer a bicycle. Stop and dismount before doors, ferry, glade observation and the falls walking switchbacks.
- Detect the actual next action through `#prompt` (including E/F), `#location`, `#clock`, `.speaker`, `.words`, `.toast`, and read-only `__haven.stats()` (`inside`, mode, position, cycling). Do not call exposed `enter`, `leave`, `interact`, `teleport`, `setTime`, controller setters or UI callbacks to substitute for inputs. Ordinary door/ferry/sit actions may internally reposition a character; record those transitions as normal gameplay.
- NPC talk takes precedence near a resident; a selected door wins only within 1.1 m of its approach interaction. Approach the named prompt, stop, then E. Other inspections can still be intercepted by a nearby resident; reposition visibly and retry rather than directly invoking an action.
- `tools/qa/walk-route.js` is optional assistance for **walking only**, declared as synthetic keyboard movement with scripted camera steering. It aborts on mode change, 5 s without .2 m improvement, or 180 s. Supply nonempty short legs; record trace/reason and release inputs after interruptions. Its initial-placement comment does not authorize teleporting any journey leg. Genuine keyboard/pointer play is preferred for this acceptance session. The observer itself writes no camera state.
