# Reed Ferry runtime candidate

2026-09-29. T1/C2/B2 candidate, not an artistic or motion approval.

`Ferry(assets, scene, collision)` clones the authored `FERRY_Reed`, `LANDING_Reed`, and `GANGWAY_Reed` roots. `update(dt, elapsedSeconds, player, inside, sceneryRange)` advances a 236-second continuous service: 18 seconds at Haven, 100 seconds to Aldermere, 18 seconds at Aldermere, 100 seconds back. Runtime uses the actual river centre samples and water heights, smooth acceleration and braking, channel turns, stowed docking oars, and small buoyancy motion. Visibility does not stop the timetable. Reload uses saved elapsed seconds. No passenger position over water should be saved as a walking spawn.

`interactions` supplies two landing prompts and a moving passenger prompt, all kind `ferry`. `interact(player)` returns dialogue or null. A parked bicycle remains at its existing position; cycling denies boarding. Boarding only starts with at least five seconds before departure. Exiting only starts while docked with at least 4.1 seconds available. The 3.9-second transition has a 3.15-second walk through the outer tread, inner floor and .62m centre aisle, plus a .75-second seating/standing phase; the passenger uses the real Character and existing blended animations. During `riding`, integration skips ordinary player locomotion, bike toggling/recovery and bench actions. Explicit recovery releases passenger control before relocation. Saving retains the last safe outdoor walking position. Interact at sea gives next-stop dialogue and cannot disembark.

Revision 2 has split passenger benches at `(±.56,.638,.82)` and raised footboards at `(±.555,.43,1.07)`. `ferryFloorHeight` supplies the same .43m support during the last walking step, rather than planting feet at the .20m central floor. `Character.contactFeet` aligns soles in the seated pose; the passenger targets are seat-local X ±.11m, forward .25m, sole Y .43m. Rower soles remain at .20m. An actual exported-geometry ray test checks the footboards within 8mm and the thin-ribbed centre floor within 2cm; actual masculine/feminine seated shoe vertices are checked within 8mm. These are mechanical contact bounds, not visual approval.

Landings are on the western bank at z=-165 and -330. Each authored platform leads south through its 2.4m gangway, then west up a supported 1.7m-wide wooden stair to dry ground. Riser height is at most .16m. Walkable support is `ferrySupportHeight(x,z)`; river collision exempts only `ferryLandingAt`. Bare rendered terrain uses `ferryGroundHeight(x,z,base)` to cut soil under the actual wooden stairs. This is necessary at the elevated Aldermere bank. These data functions import region geography, never terrainHeight. Rail collision remains resident. Deck's +X and +Z sides remain open as authored.

Maren uses the existing customizable Character, seated facing aft. The rig's animated hip is measured to place it 7cm above the bench; this is not an assumed character-root height. Animated oar handles provide world-space wrist targets for public `Character.contactHands`. The palm extends 7.5cm below the wrist target. Oar transforms use explicit yaw quaternion multiplied by roll quaternion. Rig L maps to the vessel's negative-X handle. A spinal bend with chest counterrotation brings the short arms into reach; hands release as oars stow. An actual-GLB contact test samples a complete rowing cycle and requires wrist gaps below 3.5cm. Passenger and rower posture still need close runtime inspection, as do boarding foot placement and the terrain join.
