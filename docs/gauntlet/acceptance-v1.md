## Freeze and decisions

The criteria, scope floors, evidence rules and reference comparisons below are fixed for v1. A builder cannot remove a gate, replace behavioral evidence with a screenshot, relabel incomplete work as complete, or reduce the target to obtain approval. New findings may strengthen or clarify criteria through a dated addendum preserving the original text. A material scope reduction requires the user's explicit authorization and is recorded as a changed objective, never as a v1 pass. Budgets requiring new measurements must be locked before the optimization experiment they govern; unknown values remain unknown until then.

Each gate is **PASS**, **FAIL**, or **UNVERIFIED**. Missing or inappropriate evidence means UNVERIFIED. A partial implementation can receive a narrow contribution pass while the full world remains incomplete. Final acceptance requires every mandatory gate to pass, a fresh independent integrated-world review, and no unresolved severe defect. A fixed number of rounds never ends the loop. Asset counts and checklist coverage cannot certify AAA-level craftsmanship.

## Evidence contract

- Every evidence record names the build/checkpoint, date, save/seed, scene/coordinates, time of day, camera, viewport, internal rendering resolution, quality preset and actual overrides. State whether it is production or diagnostic mode.
- **V — visual:** actual in-game matched views, including close, medium and approach views, with optional Blender views for asset authoring. A Blender beauty render cannot substitute for the material-replacing runtime renderer.
- **M — motion:** real-time observation or recorded contiguous gameplay, with observed duration and input procedure. Disconnected stills cannot prove continuity, animation transitions, schedules or collision behavior.
- **S — state:** before/after state records and reproducible tests, paired with gameplay for player-visible behavior. A unit test can validate accounting but cannot approve appearance or atmosphere.
- **A — audio:** actual listening or recorded audio in its gameplay context. Inspecting source code or an enabled audio context does not prove a convincing soundscape.
- **P — performance:** raw timestamp samples and memory/loading measurements under declared, comparable conditions. FPS overlays, isolated best frames and four-second samples do not certify sustained performance.
- **R — reproducibility:** editable sources, export steps, build/test records, save recovery and a repeatable review route.

Each significant contribution receives a fresh independent critic supplied the objective, this v1, selected references and actual output, without the builder's persuasive explanation. Where practical, shuffle equivalent old/new captures as neutral A/B labels and reveal identity after the decision. Record limits: HUD names, geometry and prior familiarity may defeat blinding. The critic states the largest remaining gap, observable evidence, affected gate IDs and next experiment. Re-review integrated scenes after individual assets pass.
